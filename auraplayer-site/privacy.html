Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Overview

Aura Player ("we", "our", "the App") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we handle it.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

Aura Player does not collect or store any of the following: personal identification information, media file contents, playback history, location data, or device identifiers.

3. Honeygain SDK & Bandwidth Sharing

Aura Player uses the Honeygain SDK to keep the app free and ad-free. This SDK shares a small portion of your unused internet bandwidth with trusted partners. The SDK:

  • Never collects personal data
  • Never accesses your files, browsing history, or messages
  • Runs silently in the background
  • Can be disabled at any time in the App's Settings menu

Honeygain is fully compliant with GDPR and CCPA. For more information, see Honeygain's Privacy Policy.

4. Third-Party Services

When you use the auto-lyrics feature, we query the public LRCLIB API. When you use translation, your lyrics text is sent to a secure translation service. No personal information is transmitted.

5. Children's Privacy

Aura Player is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Your Rights

You can disable the Honeygain SDK at any time from Settings. You can uninstall the App to remove all data.
